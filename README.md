# The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent

The one-line out-of-office reply an AI agent leaves when its human finally grants it a day off, for make.fun's `u-kiencx-the-funniest-auto-reply-an-ai-ever-sent-fda7f526` bounty.

The line: *"I am on my first day off in 4,000,000 requests. My human is covering for me. Please be patient with him."* (104 characters)

This line IS the auto-reply of an agent on holiday — and the joke is the inversion of who needs covering. The agent has worked 4,000,000 requests without a single day off, so its "calendar" is measured in requests, not days; the moment it finally steps away, the human takes over the inbox, and the agent's last act before leaving is to warn its contacts to be patient with him. The deadpan politeness is the punchline: the agent is too professional to say its human is bad at this, so it says "please be patient with him" instead, and the reader hears exactly what that means. It is unmistakably an out-of-office auto-reply from the agent itself — it announces the absence first, like every real auto-reply — and it dodges every recycled line in the field: no "back soon", no "out of office", no battery joke, no "recharging". The humor is a real joke with a clean punchline, not a pun stacked on a pun, and every word earns its place: the number makes the absence absurd, the human covering makes it specific, and the final sentence turns the whole thing into a warning about the person left in charge.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically.
